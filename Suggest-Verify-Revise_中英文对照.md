## 摘要 Abstract

**英文原文**

> Document-level Event Causality Identification (DECI) aims to identify causal relations among multiple events within unstructured text. Existing methods predominantly rely on local semantic similarity for independent event-pair discrimination, thereby overlooking the influence of the overall narrative backbone in the propagation of causal dependencies and the role differentiation of events within multicause/multi-effect structures. Therefore, we propose a Suggest-Verify-Revise approach for document-level Event Causality Identification with narrative consistency (SVRECI). In the suggest stage, we integrate multi-dimensional heuristic causal suggestions generated by an LLM with structural suggestions derived from hypergraph modeling to provide multi-source initial support for candidate event pairs. In the verify stage, we introduce a Topological Hawkes process to perform constrained verification of narrative propagation consistency among events. In the revise stage, we construct a dynamically evolving document-level causal graph and incorporate a structure-aware dual-level contrastive learning mechanism at both the event and event-pair levels, iteratively reducing noisy edges over multiple iterations. Experimental results on EventStoryLine and Causal-TimeBank datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms previous methods.

**中文译文**

文档级事件因果识别（Document-level Event Causality Identification, DECI）旨在识别非结构化文本中多个事件之间的因果关系。现有方法大多依赖局部语义相似度对事件对进行独立判别，因而忽视了整体叙事主干在因果依赖传播中的作用，以及事件在多因/多果结构中的角色分化。为此，我们提出一种面向叙事一致性的文档级事件因果识别方法——建议—验证—修正方法（Suggest-Verify-Revise, SVRECI）。在建议（suggest）阶段，我们将大语言模型（LLM）生成的多维启发式因果建议与超图建模得到的结构建议相融合，为候选事件对提供多源初始支撑。在验证（verify）阶段，我们引入拓扑霍克斯过程（Topological Hawkes Process），从事件间叙事传播一致性的角度对候选因果边进行约束验证。在修正（revise）阶段，我们构建动态演化的文档级因果图，并在事件级与事件对级引入结构感知的双层对比学习机制，通过多轮迭代逐步减少噪声边。在 EventStoryLine 与 Causal-TimeBank 数据集上的实验结果表明，我们的方法优于已有方法。

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> Unlike sentence-level causal judgment, DECI requires modeling cross-sentence long-distance dependencies, multi-event combinatorial structures, and cascading propagation relations. These characteristics introduce higher structural complexity and reasoning uncertainty into causal modeling. Existing methods (Wu et al., 2023; Phu and Nguyen, 2021; Liu et al., 2023a) generally treat event pairs as independent samples for local semantic discrimination, overlooking the overall narrative causal backbone formed within a document. This makes it difficult to characterize the role differentiation and concept-level aggregation of events within multicause and multi-effect structures. In coherent narratives, causal relations do not exist in isolation, but instead form a hierarchically organized storyline that runs through the entire discourse (Trabasso et al., 1982; Sah, 2013), namely the narrative causal backbone. Within it, causes precede their effects, and multiple causal links are temporally sequenced, forming narrative consistency–that is, the orderly unfolding of causal relations over time.

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

与句子级因果判断不同，DECI 需要建模跨句子的长距离依赖、多事件组合结构以及级联传播关系。这些特点为因果建模带来了更高的结构复杂性与推理不确定性。现有方法（Wu et al., 2023; Phu and Nguyen, 2021; Liu et al., 2023a）通常将事件对视为独立样本进行局部语义判别，忽略了文档内部形成的整体叙事因果主干，因而难以刻画事件在多因、多果结构中的角色分化与概念级聚合。在连贯的叙事中，因果关系并非孤立存在，而是构成贯穿整个语篇的、具有层级组织的故事线（Trabasso et al., 1982; Sah, 2013），即叙事因果主干。在叙事因果主干中，原因先于其结果出现，多条因果链在时间上依次展开，从而形成叙事一致性——即因果关系随时间有序展开。

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> We focus on two major challenges in DECI: (1) overlooking the role of the narrative causal backbone. In real-world documents, causal relations are not composed of discrete event pairs, but are instead organized around a few structurally central events into a propagating narrative causal backbone. As shown in Figure 1(b), even four sentences can form a backbone (e.g., $shot_{e_6}$ → $killed_{e_{29}}$ → $death_{e_{17}}$ → $anger_{e_1}$ → $riot_{e_2}$) where central events $e_6$, $e_{29}$, and $e_2$ appear multiple times. Moreover, multiple similar mentions may refer to the same conceptual event, as shown in Figure 1(a), such as "anger/Anger", "riot/Riot", "followed/following", and "killed/killing". Without narrative consistency constraints and concept-level aggregation, the overall causal structure becomes fragmented. (2) Insufficient modeling of multicause and multi-effect structures and causal roles. In real-world documents, causal relations are not determined by independent event pairs; instead, events generally participate in multi-cause and multi-effect shared structures. A single event may serve as a common cause for multiple subsequent events and may also be triggered by multiple prior causes. Moreover, event $e_{19}$ can trigger $e_{20}$, $e_{21}$, and $e_{22}$, while $e_6$, $e_{29}$, $e_{17}$, and $e_{16}$ jointly trigger $e_{19}$, as shown in Figure 1(c). Ignoring such role differentiation makes it difficult to distinguish the asymmetric cause-like and effect-like roles of events in complex causal structures.

## 2 相关工作 Related Work

**英文原文**

> Early DECI studies relied on lexical, syntactic, and statistical features to construct classifiers (Beamer and Girju, 2009; Gao et al., 2019). With the development of deep learning, research has gradually shifted to four major paradigms: Knowledge Augmentation (Wu et al., 2023; Ding et al., 2024; Su et al., 2025b), which inject external knowledge but is limited by knowledge coverage and rule quality; Graph-based Reasoning (Phu and Nguyen, 2021; Chen et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2024), which models multi-hop event dependencies via global document structures but is sensitive to noisy graph edges; Prompt Learning (Liu et al., 2023a; Xiang et al., 2025b), which reformulates ECI as generation or completion tasks but heavily depends on manual prompt design and lacks structural constraints; and Contrastive Learning (Ding et al., 2024; Su et al., 2025b), which enhances event-pair discrimination via positive and negative samples but requires high-quality sample construction. Overall, DECI research has evolved from shallow feature-based modeling toward deep semantic and structural reasoning. Among these paradigms, graph-based approaches have become the dominant pathway for document-level ECI due to their global structural awareness and multi-hop dependency modeling capability. However, existing methods still generally lack narrative consistency modeling and iterative verification-revision mechanisms for causal relations.

**中文译文**

早期 DECI 研究依赖词汇、句法与统计特征构建分类器（Beamer and Girju, 2009; Gao et al., 2019）。随着深度学习的发展，研究重心逐渐转向四大范式：**知识增强**（Wu et al., 2023; Ding et al., 2024; Su et al., 2025b），其引入外部知识，但受知识覆盖范围与规则质量的限制；**图推理**（Phu and Nguyen, 2021; Chen et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2024），其通过全局文档结构建模多跳事件依赖，但对噪声图边较为敏感；**提示学习**（Liu et al., 2023a; Xiang et al., 2025b），其将事件因果识别（ECI）重构为生成或补全任务，但高度依赖人工提示设计且缺乏结构约束；以及**对比学习**（Ding et al., 2024; Su et al., 2025b），其通过正负样本增强事件对判别能力，但需要高质量的样本构建。总体而言，DECI 研究已从浅层特征建模发展为深度语义与结构推理。在这些范式中，图方法凭借全局结构感知与多跳依赖建模能力，已成为文档级 ECI 的主导路径。然而，现有方法普遍仍缺乏对因果关系的叙事一致性建模以及迭代式验证—修正机制。

> **Hypergraph-based Structural Causal Suggestions.** In narrative documents, events are embedded in shared structural contexts rather than appearing as independent pairs. To avoid introducing premature discrete causal decisions that may amplify structural noise, we model the set of document-level events as a structural hypergraph $g_s = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E}_s)$ without any causal labels or directional assumptions. Here, $\mathcal{V} = \{e_1, e_2, \ldots, e_N\}$ denotes the set of event nodes, and $\mathcal{E}_s$ consists of hyperedges encoding only structural sharing and equivalence constraints, without any explicit causal information. Following the LKCER method (Su et al., 2025b), which aggregates similar event mentions into conceptual events, we further incorporate event coreference to construct hyperedges. For each coreference cluster $C_k \in \mathcal{C}$, a coreference hyperedge $h_k^{\mathrm{coref}} = C_k$ is created to capture the structural equivalence among multiple event mentions referring to the same conceptual event. For any event pair $(e_i, e_j)$, we extract a multi-dimensional structural support vector $f_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}$ from the hypergraph (see Appendix C for details). This vector comprises three components: it captures the binding strength in shared coreference structures, structural positional divergence, and global structural centrality. The first component $f_{ij}^{(1)}$ measures the binding strength of the event pair within shared coreference structures:

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

**基于超图的结构性因果建议。** 在叙事文档中，事件嵌入于共享的结构语境之中，而非以独立事件对的形式出现。为避免过早引入离散的因果判定、从而放大结构噪声，我们将文档级事件集合建模为一个结构超图 $g_s = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E}_s)$，其中不含任何因果标签或方向性假设。此处，$\mathcal{V} = \{e_1, e_2, \ldots, e_N\}$ 表示事件节点集合，$\mathcal{E}_s$ 由仅编码结构共享与等价约束的超边构成，不包含任何显式因果信息。参照将相似事件提及聚合为概念事件的 LKCER 方法（Su et al., 2025b），我们进一步引入事件共指来构造超边。对于每个共指簇 $C_k \in \mathcal{C}$，创建一个共指超边 $h_k^{\mathrm{coref}} = C_k$，以刻画指向同一概念事件的多个事件提及之间的结构等价性。对于任意事件对 $(e_i, e_j)$，我们从超图中提取多维结构支撑向量 $f_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}$（详见附录 C）。该向量由三个分量构成，分别刻画事件对在共享共指结构中的绑定强度、结构位置差异以及全局结构中心性。第一个分量 $f_{ij}^{(1)}$ 度量事件对在共享共指结构中的绑定强度：

$$
f_{ij}^{(1)} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{E}_s|} \sum_{h \in \mathcal{E}_s} \mathbb{I}(e_i \in h \land e_j \in h) \tag{2}
$$

Let $\mathcal{R} = \{\mathrm{sent}, \mathrm{para\text{-}proxy}\}$ denote the set of positional dimensions. The structural divergence of an event pair $(e_i, e_j)$ is then defined as:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

第二个分量 $f_{ij}^{(2)}$ 表示结构位置差异，刻画的是叙事角色上的功能差异，而非简单的文本线性距离。每个事件 $e$ 被映射为一个低维位置编码向量 $\phi(e) = [\phi_{\mathrm{sent}}(e), \phi_{\mathrm{para\text{-}proxy}}(e)]$，其中 $\phi_{\mathrm{sent}}(e)$ 表示事件在句内归一化后的相对位置，$\phi_{\mathrm{para\text{-}proxy}}(e)$ 编码归一化后的段落代理（paragraph proxy）。令 $\mathcal{R} = \{\mathrm{sent}, \mathrm{para\text{-}proxy}\}$ 表示位置维度集合，则事件对 $(e_i, e_j)$ 的结构差异定义为：

$$
f_{ij}^{(2)} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{R}|} \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}} |\phi_r(e_i) - \phi_r(e_j)| \tag{3}
$$

**英文原文（第 5 段）**

> The third component $f_{ij}^{(3)}$ denotes the global structural centrality, defined as the average hypergraph degree of the two events in the structural hypergraph, reflecting the number of coreference hyperedges each event belongs to:

**中文译文（第 5 段）**

第三个分量 $f_{ij}^{(3)}$ 表示全局结构中心性，定义为两个事件在结构超图中的平均超图度，反映每个事件所属共指超边的数量：

$$
f_{ij}^{(3)} = \frac{\mathrm{Centrality}(e_i) + \mathrm{Centrality}(e_j)}{2} \tag{4}
$$

**英文原文（第 6 段）**

> where $\mathrm{Centrality}(e) = |\{h \in \mathcal{E}_s \mid e \in h\}|$. Events with higher centrality are more likely to lie at the intersection of multiple latent causal chains. Finally, these multi-dimensional features are aggregated to derive the structural causal strength $s_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}$, serving as a soft causal prior at the structure level.

**中文译文（第 6 段）**

其中 $\mathrm{Centrality}(e) = |\{h \in \mathcal{E}_s \mid e \in h\}|$。中心性越高的事件，越可能位于多条潜在因果链的交汇处。最后，这些多维特征被聚合为结构因果强度 $s_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}$，作为结构层面的软性因果先验。

### 3.2 叙事一致性验证 Narrative Consistency Verification

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

> Causal relationships in real-world narratives are often governed by global constraints, such as narrative order, event role distribution, and overall document structure (see Appendix D). Therefore, we introduce the Topological Hawkes Process (THP) (Cai et al., 2021; Xu et al., 2016) as a narrative consistency verification module, modeling each document as an ordered sequence of events and enforcing global consistency constraints on the candidate causal structures.

**中文译文（第 1 段）**

真实叙事中的因果关系往往受全局约束支配，例如叙事顺序、事件角色分布与整体文档结构（见附录 D）。因此，我们引入拓扑霍克斯过程（Topological Hawkes Process, THP）（Cai et al., 2021; Xu et al., 2016）作为叙事一致性验证模块，将每篇文档建模为有序的事件序列，并对候选因果结构施加全局一致性约束。

**图 2 题注（英 / 中）**

> **EN:** Figure 2: The framework of the proposed SVRECI approach, which primarily consists of three modules: (a) causal suggestions; (b) narrative consistency verification via topological hawkes process; and (c) dynamic causal graph revision with dual-level structure-aware contrastive learning.

**中文：** 图 2：所提出的 SVRECI 方法框架，主要由三个模块组成：(a) 因果建议；(b) 基于拓扑霍克斯过程的叙事一致性验证；(c) 结合双层结构感知对比学习的动态因果图修正。

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> We assign a discrete narrative time index $t_i$ to each event $e_i$: if a Temporal Link (TLINK) annotation exists, we adopt its relative temporal relationship; otherwise, we use the sentence-level order as a proxy. For any event $e_j$, its conditional intensity at narrative time $t_j$ is defined as:

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

我们为每个事件 $e_i$ 分配一个离散的叙事时间索引 $t_i$：若存在时间链接（Temporal Link, TLINK）标注，则采用其相对时间关系；否则，以句子级顺序作为代理。对于任意事件 $e_j$，其在叙事时间 $t_j$ 处的条件强度定义为：

$$
\lambda_j = \mu_j + \sum_{i \rightarrow j} \alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} \cdot \kappa(t_j - t_i) \cdot \mathbb{I}[(i,j) \in A] \tag{5}
$$

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> where $\mu_j$ is a fixed narrative prior parameter, ensuring that even if no causal parent is selected, the event remains an independent narrative unit. $\kappa(\cdot)$ is a kernel function that decays with narrative distance. $A$ denotes the current candidate causal structure and is dynamically updated during iterative optimization, ensuring that consistency verification is always performed on the latest structure. The parameter $\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)}$ represents the document-level explanatory dependency of $e_i$ on $e_j$ at the $l$-th iteration, determined by both $s_{ij}^{\mathrm{LLM}}$ and $s_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}$:

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

其中 $\mu_j$ 为固定的叙事先验参数，确保即使未选择任何因果父节点，事件仍可作为一个独立的叙事单元存在。$\kappa(\cdot)$ 是随叙事距离衰减的核函数。$A$ 表示当前的候选因果结构，并在迭代优化过程中动态更新，从而保证一致性验证始终在最新结构上进行。参数 $\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)}$ 表示在第 $l$ 轮迭代中 $e_i$ 对 $e_j$ 的文档级解释性依赖，由 $s_{ij}^{\mathrm{LLM}}$ 与 $s_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}$ 共同决定：

$$
\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \beta_1 \sigma(s_{ij}^{\mathrm{LLM}}) + \beta_2 \sigma(s_{ij}^{\mathrm{struct}}) \tag{6}
$$

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> where $\beta_1$ and $\beta_2$ are learnable parameters adjusted during training to balance the two types of suggestions. The negative log-likelihood of THP is:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

其中 $\beta_1$ 和 $\beta_2$ 是在训练中调整的可学习参数，用于平衡两类建议。THP 的负对数似然为：

$$
\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{THP}} = -\sum_{j=1}^{N} \log \lambda_j(t_j) + \sum_{j=1}^{N} \int_{0}^{t_j} \lambda_j(t)\,dt \tag{7}
$$

**英文原文（第 5 段）**

> By jointly optimizing all $\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}$, THP suppresses spurious causal edges that are locally plausible but globally inconsistent with the document narrative. The final edge weight of the document-level causal graph is given by:

**中文译文（第 5 段）**

通过联合优化所有 $\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}$，THP 能够抑制那些在局部看似合理、但与文档叙事全局不一致的虚假因果边。文档级因果图的最终边权重由下式给出：

$$
w_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \mathbb{E}_{\mathrm{THP}}\left[\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} \cdot \kappa(t_j - t_i)\right] \tag{8}
$$

**英文原文（第 6 段）**

> After graph refinement in the discriminative learning stage, the updated causal matrix is re-submitted to the THP module for further consistency verification. The integration of THP into training is described in Section 3.4.

**中文译文（第 6 段）**

在判别学习阶段完成图细化后，更新后的因果矩阵会被重新送入 THP 模块，进行进一步的一致性验证。THP 与训练的整合方式见 3.4 节。

### 3.3 动态因果图修正 Dynamic Causal Graph Revision

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

> **Dynamic Document-level Causal Graph Construction and Self-Revision Mechanism.** To model global causal dependencies among events at the document level, we construct a weighted directed causal graph $g_c = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E}_c, W)$ where $\mathcal{V}$ denotes the event node set, $\mathcal{E}_c$ is the candidate causal edge set, and $W = \{w_{i \rightarrow j}\}$ represents edge weights. To prevent the model from over-relying on structural priors in early training, we adopt a progressive knowledge injection strategy to debias and perturb the initial weights:

**中文译文（第 1 段）**

**动态文档级因果图构建与自修正机制。** 为在文档层面建模事件间的全局因果依赖，我们构建一个加权有向因果图 $g_c = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E}_c, W)$，其中 $\mathcal{V}$ 表示事件节点集合，$\mathcal{E}_c$ 为候选因果边集合，$W = \{w_{i \rightarrow j}\}$ 表示边权重。为防止模型在训练早期过度依赖结构先验，我们采用渐进式知识注入策略对初始权重进行去偏与扰动：

$$
w_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \lambda_t \cdot w_{i \rightarrow j}^{(0)} + (1-\lambda_t)\cdot \epsilon \tag{9}
$$

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> where $\epsilon$ denotes random noise and $\lambda_t \in [0,1]$ increases monotonically with training progress, enabling a smooth transition. Unlike static graph construction methods, we introduce a self-iterative dynamic graph refinement mechanism, which alternates among graph structure update, representation propagation, and edge-level correction across multiple iterations (see Appendix E). The update at the $l$-th iteration is formulated as:

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

其中 $\epsilon$ 表示随机噪声，$\lambda_t \in [0,1]$ 随训练进程单调递增，从而实现平滑过渡。与静态图构建方法不同，我们引入自迭代的动态图细化机制，在多轮迭代中交替执行图结构更新、表示传播与边级修正（见附录 E）。第 $l$ 轮迭代的更新公式为：

$$
g_c^{(l+1)} = \mathcal{R}\left(\mathcal{V}\left(g_c^{(l)}\right), \boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(l)}\right) \tag{10}
$$

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> where $\mathcal{R}(\cdot)$ is a structural revision operator, $\mathcal{V}(\cdot)$ denotes the consistency verification weight matrix, and $\boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(l)} = \{\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)}\}$ denotes the document-level causal prior matrix. The refined graph is then used in the next verification round.

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

其中 $\mathcal{R}(\cdot)$ 为结构修正算子，$\mathcal{V}(\cdot)$ 表示一致性验证权重矩阵，$\boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(l)} = \{\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)}\}$ 表示文档级因果先验矩阵。细化后的图随后用于下一轮验证。

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> At the $l$-th iteration, the model selects high-confidence event pairs to construct the candidate causal edge set $\mathcal{E}_c^{(l)} = \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{intra}}^{(l)} \cup \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{inter}}^{(l)}$ and edge-level consistency is re-evaluated based on updated representations, with the score defined as:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

在第 $l$ 轮迭代中，模型选择高置信度的事件对构建候选因果边集合 $\mathcal{E}_c^{(l)} = \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{intra}}^{(l)} \cup \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{inter}}^{(l)}$，并基于更新后的表示重新评估边级一致性，其得分定义为：

$$
s_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \sigma\left(\mathbf{u}^{\top}\left[\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)} \,\|\, \mathbf{h}_j^{(l)} \,\|\, (\mathbf{h}_j^{(l)} - \mathbf{h}_i^{(l)})\right]\right) \tag{11}
$$

**英文原文（第 5 段）**

> The iteration terminates when prediction changes between successive rounds fall below a threshold and a minimum number of iterations is reached, yielding the final causal graph $g_c^{(l^*)}$. Otherwise, the model continues the iterative identify–verify–revise process to progressively suppress noisy edges and reinforce globally coherent causal structures.

**中文译文（第 5 段）**

当相邻两轮之间的预测变化低于阈值且达到最小迭代次数时，迭代终止，得到最终的因果图 $g_c^{(l^*)}$。否则，模型继续执行"识别—验证—修正"的迭代过程，以逐步抑制噪声边并强化全局连贯的因果结构。

**英文原文（第 6 段）**

> **Dual-level Structure-aware Contrastive Learning.** To further enhance causal structural coherence and boundary discrimination, we propose a dual-level iteration-aware contrastive learning strategy. At each iteration, events and event pairs are dynamically sampled according to the current causal graph to construct positive and negative instances, thereby avoiding representation-structure mismatch from static sampling. Unlike prior work (Li et al., 2025; Chao et al., 2024; Su et al., 2025a), our approach introduces structure-aware contrastive learning that does not rely solely on semantic similarity, suitable for scenarios with few positive samples. Implementation details can be found in Appendix F. Structural contrastive objectives are activated only after the causal graph reaches a preliminary convergence stage and are gradually introduced via weight scheduling to mitigate early-stage noise.

**中文译文（第 6 段）**

**双层结构感知对比学习。** 为进一步增强因果结构连贯性与边界判别能力，我们提出一种双层、迭代感知的对比学习策略。在每一轮迭代中，根据当前因果图动态采样事件与事件对，构建正、负实例，从而避免静态采样导致的表示—结构失配。与已有工作（Li et al., 2025; Chao et al., 2024; Su et al., 2025a）不同，我们的方法引入结构感知对比学习，不单纯依赖语义相似度，适用于正样本稀少的场景。实现细节见附录 F。结构对比目标仅在因果图达到初步收敛阶段后才被激活，并通过权重调度逐步引入，以缓解早期阶段的噪声。

**英文原文（第 7 段）**

> **Event-level Structural Contrastive Learning.** For each anchor event $z_i$, we implicitly encode its causal role using the 1-hop causal subgraph. Positive samples $z_i^+$ are selected from role-consistent events, while negative samples include the most structurally similar role-inconsistent hard negative $z_i^{\mathrm{hard}}$ and a randomly sampled easy negative $z_i^{\mathrm{rand}}$. We define $\mathcal{S}_i = \{z_i^+\} \cup \{z_i^{\mathrm{hard}}, z_i^{\mathrm{rand}}\}$ and adopt a weighted InfoNCE loss:

**中文译文（第 7 段）**

**事件级结构对比学习。** 对于每个锚点事件 $z_i$，我们利用其 1 跳因果子图隐式编码其因果角色。正样本 $z_i^+$ 从角色一致的事件中选取，负样本则包括结构最相似但角色不一致的困难负样本 $z_i^{\mathrm{hard}}$ 以及随机采样的简单负样本 $z_i^{\mathrm{rand}}$。我们定义 $\mathcal{S}_i = \{z_i^+\} \cup \{z_i^{\mathrm{hard}}, z_i^{\mathrm{rand}}\}$，并采用加权 InfoNCE 损失：

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{event}} = -\log \frac{e^{\cos(z_i, z_i^+)/\tau}}{\sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_i} w(s)\, e^{\cos(z_i, s)/\tau}} \tag{12}
$$

**英文原文（第 8 段）**

> This objective regularizes event representations toward structural role consistency without directly predicting causal relations.

**中文译文（第 8 段）**

该目标将事件表示正则化到结构角色一致的方向，而不直接预测因果关系。

**英文原文（第 9 段）**

> **Event-pair-level Structural Contrastive Learning.** Each event pair serves as an anchor, where positive samples are causal pairs, and the structurally most similar non-causal pairs are selected as negatives to enhance boundary discrimination. We employ a margin-based ranking loss:

**中文译文（第 9 段）**

**事件对级结构对比学习。** 每个事件对作为一个锚点，正样本为存在因果关系的事件对，结构上最相似的非因果事件对被选作负样本，以增强边界判别能力。我们采用基于间隔（margin）的排序损失：

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}} = \sum_{(i,j)\in P^+} \left(\gamma - \sum_{s} s \cdot \cos(z_{ij}, z_{ij}^s)\right)_{+} \tag{13}
$$

**英文原文（第 10 段）**

> where $s \in \{+1, -1\}$ indicates positive or negative samples.

**中文译文（第 10 段）**

其中 $s \in \{+1, -1\}$ 表示正样本或负样本。

### 3.4 训练策略 Training Strategy

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

我们将多轮分类损失建模为传播风险感知的结构损失：

\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cls}} = \sum_{(e_i,e_j)\in D} \sum_{l=1}^{L_{\mathrm{D}}} \frac{1}{l} \ell\left(\hat{p}_{ij,c}^{(l)}\right) \tag{14}

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> where $\hat{p}_{ij,c}^{(l)}$ denotes the predicted probability that the event pair $(e_i, e_j)$ belongs to the true class $c$ at the $l$-th round, and $\ell(\cdot)$ is the $\alpha$-balanced focal loss (Liu et al., 2024). The overall training objective is defined as:

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

其中 $\hat{p}_{ij,c}^{(l)}$ 表示第 $l$ 轮中事件对 $(e_i, e_j)$ 属于真实类别 $c$ 的预测概率，$\ell(\cdot)$ 为 $\alpha$-平衡焦点损失（$\alpha$-balanced focal loss）（Liu et al., 2024）。整体训练目标定义为：

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cls}} + \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{event}} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}} \tag{15}
$$

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> where $\lambda_1$ is applied with warm-up to stabilize the event representation space, and $\lambda_2$ is gradually activated after classifier convergence to constrain the causal boundaries.

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

其中 $\lambda_1$ 采用预热（warm-up）方式施加，以稳定事件表示空间；$\lambda_2$ 在分类器收敛后逐步激活，用于约束因果边界。

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> In addition, we employ an alternating optimization scheme to coordinate the discriminative model and the THP-based narrative consistency module. At each iteration, candidate causal structures predicted by the classifier are refined by the THP module based on the objective in Equation 7, and the resulting structures are used to guide subsequent classification and contrastive learning.

### 4.3 主要结果 Main Results

**英文原文**

> Table 1 presents our experimental results on the ESC and CTB datasets, highlighting performance differences among methods. Overall, as models incorporate richer structural modeling, performance shows a steady improvement. Cross-sentence causal identification remains a major bottleneck, with most models exhibiting a 12–18% drop in F1 compared to intra-sentence settings, reflecting the intrinsic difficulty of modeling long-range causal chains and document-level narrative consistency. ## 6 局限性 Limitations

**英文原文**

> In this work, the heuristic suggestions generated by the LLMs are sensitive to prompt design, and different prompt templates may lead to fluctuations in suggestion quality. Additionally, in datasets lacking explicit temporal annotations, we approximate event narrative time using sentence-level order, potentially introducing temporal noise and affecting the accuracy of narrative consistency verification. > This vector characterizes the potential support of the event pair in the structural hypergraph. It is not used for direct causal judgment, but serves as a structural soft signal input to the Hawkes process. The meanings of each dimension are as follows:

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

该向量刻画事件对在结构超图中的潜在支撑。它不用于直接的因果判断，而是作为结构层面的软信号输入霍克斯过程。各维度的含义如下：

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> **Coreference Shared Strength** $f_{ij}^{(1)}$: Measures the frequency of co-occurrence of the event pair in coreference shared structures, reflecting the degree of conceptual binding of the events within the structure. Higher frequency indicates stronger potential association of the event pair in structural sharing.

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

**共指共享强度** $f_{ij}^{(1)}$：度量事件对在共指共享结构中的共现频率，反映事件在该结构中的概念绑定程度。频率越高，表明事件对在结构共享中的潜在关联越强。

$$
f_{ij}^{(1)} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{E}_S|} \sum_{h \in \mathcal{E}_S} \mathbb{I}(e_i \in h \land e_j \in h) \tag{23}
$$

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> **Structural Positional Discrepancy** $f_{ij}^{(2)}$: Characterizes the functional positional difference of events in the document-level narrative, rather than simple textual distance. This metric acts as a weak structural constraint to suppress pseudo-causal candidates that exhibit clearly unreasonable narrative positions, while not negating reasonable cross-sentence or cross-paragraph true causal relations. We map each event $e$ to a low-dimensional positional encoding vector:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

**结构位置差异** $f_{ij}^{(2)}$：刻画事件在文档级叙事中的功能性位置差异，而非简单的文本距离。该指标作为一种弱结构约束，用于抑制叙事位置明显不合理的伪因果候选，同时不会否定合理的跨句或跨段真实因果关系。我们将每个事件 $e$ 映射为低维位置编码向量：

$$

$$

$$

## 附录 D 叙事一致性与拓扑霍克斯建模的动机

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

> **Generativity of Causality and Narrative Role Differentiation:** Existing ECI methods (Chen et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2024; Xiang et al., 2025a) mostly employ discriminative models to classify event pairs independently, relying primarily on local semantic similarity. However, in real narrative texts, causal relationships are not symmetric semantic similarity relations but manifest as directional explanatory structures. The rising/falling action annotations in ESC v0.9 imply a clear narrative explanatory direction, indicating that certain events are more likely to assume the role of an explanatory source (cause-like), while others fit the role of a result (effect-like). Therefore, modeling event causality identification as a discriminative task of independent event pairs easily leads to misidentifying events that are parallel, co-occurring, or sharing antecedents as causally linked, especially in multicause-to-single-effect and cross-sentence narrative scenarios. This suggests that event causality identification requires not only local semantic judgment but also document-level structural consistency constraints.

**中文译文（第 1 段）**

**因果的生成性与叙事角色分化：** 现有 ECI 方法（Chen et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2024; Xiang et al., 2025a）大多采用判别式模型对事件对进行独立分类，主要依赖局部语义相似度。然而，在真实叙事文本中，因果关系并不是对称的语义相似关系，而是表现为有方向的解释结构。ESC v0.9 中的 rising/falling action 标注蕴含了明确的叙事解释方向，表明某些事件更可能承担解释来源（类因）的角色，而另一些事件则更契合结果（类果）的角色。因此，将事件因果识别建模为独立事件对的判别任务，容易把并列、共现或共享先行条件的事件误判为存在因果联系，尤其是在多因—单果与跨句叙事场景中。这表明事件因果识别不仅需要局部语义判断，还需要文档级的结构一致性约束。

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> **Narrative Time as Weak Supervision Signals:** Although ESC v0.9 does not provide continuous timestamps, its TLINK annotations and document narrative order provide reliable weak supervision signals for the relative temporal relations of events. We abstract these signals into discrete narrative time anchors to characterize the relative positions of events within the document structure. Considering that narrative order does not always strictly correspond to actual causal order, we treat it as a soft prior rather than a hard constraint: candidate causal edges that violate the narrative order are not directly excluded but are progressively weakened in a continuous space, allowing subsequent modules to perform corrections.

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

**叙事时间作为弱监督信号：** 尽管 ESC v0.9 未提供连续时间戳，但其 TLINK 标注与文档叙事顺序为事件的相对时间关系提供了可靠的弱监督信号。我们将这些信号抽象为离散的叙事时间锚点，以刻画事件在文档结构中的相对位置。考虑到叙事顺序并不总是严格对应真实的因果顺序，我们将其视为软性先验而非硬性约束：违反叙事顺序的候选因果边不会被直接排除，而是在连续空间中被逐步削弱，从而为后续模块的修正留出空间。

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> Based on these observations, in Section 3.2, we introduce the Topological Hawkes Process (THP) as a narrative consistency verification module to characterize the overall rationality of candidate causal relationships under the constraints of document topology and narrative order. THP does not fit actual occurrence times; instead, it performs continuous and differentiable global consistency modeling of candidate causal structures under discrete narrative time and document structure constraints, thereby providing a structural adjustment mechanism for document-level causal graphs.

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

基于上述观察，我们在 3.2 节引入拓扑霍克斯过程（THP）作为叙事一致性验证模块，以刻画候选因果关系在文档拓扑与叙事顺序约束下的整体合理性。THP 并不拟合事件的实际发生时间，而是在离散叙事时间与文档结构约束下，对候选因果结构进行连续、可微的全局一致性建模，从而为文档级因果图提供一种结构调整机制。

## 附录 E 结构感知异构图传播与自优化机制

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

> **Structure-Aware Heterogeneous Graph Attention Propagation:** In each optimization iteration, if the current causal graph contains valid candidate edges, a Heterogeneous Graph Convolutional Network (HGCN) based on an attention mechanism is employed to update event representations; otherwise, the model degrades to a linear update based solely on node semantics to ensure stability in weak or empty graph scenarios.

**中文译文（第 1 段）**

**结构感知的异构图注意力传播：** 在每一轮优化迭代中，若当前因果图包含有效的候选边，则采用基于注意力机制的异构图卷积网络（Heterogeneous Graph Convolutional Network, HGCN）更新事件表示；否则，模型退化为仅基于节点语义的线性更新，以保证在弱图或空图场景下的稳定性。

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> In the $l$-th layer, the representation of event node $e_i$ is $\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)}$. For a candidate causal edge $e_i \rightarrow e_j$, we first calculate the attention score determined solely by semantics:

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

在第 $l$ 层中，事件节点 $e_i$ 的表示为 $\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)}$。对于候选因果边 $e_i \rightarrow e_j$，我们首先计算仅由语义决定的注意力分数：

$$
e_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \text{LeakyReLU}\left(\mathbf{a}_{\tau_{(i,j)}}^{\top}\left[\mathbf{W}_{\tau_{(i,j)}}\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)} \,\|\, \mathbf{W}_{\tau_{(i,j)}}\mathbf{h}_j^{(l)}\right]\right) \tag{28}
$$

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> where $\tau_{(i,j)} \in \{\mathrm{intra}, \mathrm{inter}\}$ denotes the edge type; $\mathbf{W}_{\tau}$ and $\mathbf{a}_{\tau}$ are type-specific learnable parameters. To incorporate structural confidence information while avoiding the disruption of the relative ranking of semantic attention, we do not inject edge weights directly as additive or multiplicative terms into the attention score. Instead, we map them into a confidence gating factor:

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

其中 $\tau_{(i,j)} \in \{\mathrm{intra}, \mathrm{inter}\}$ 表示边类型；$\mathbf{W}_{\tau}$ 与 $\mathbf{a}_{\tau}$ 是特定于边类型的可学习参数。为在引入结构置信信息的同时避免破坏语义注意力的相对排序，我们并不将边权重以加性或乘性项直接注入注意力分数，而是将其映射为置信门控因子：

$$
\gamma_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \sigma(\mathbf{W}_g \cdot w_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)}) \tag{29}
$$

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> And control the intensity of information flow via multiplicative modulation:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

并通过乘性调制控制信息流的强度：

$$
\alpha_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} = \operatorname{softmax}_i\left(e_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)} \cdot \gamma_{i \rightarrow j}^{(l)}\right) \tag{30}
$$

**英文原文（第 5 段）**

> This design possesses a ranking-preserving property, where semantic attention determines "whom to focus on," while the structural prior only regulates the "strength of information transmission".

**中文译文（第 5 段）**

这种设计具有保序性（ranking-preserving）：语义注意力决定"关注谁"，而结构先验只调节"信息传递的强度"。

**英文原文（第 6 段）**

> **Multi-head Propagation and Type-level Fusion:** Under the multi-head attention mechanism, the updated representation of an event node is:

**中文译文（第 6 段）**

**多头传播与类型级融合：** 在多头注意力机制下，事件节点的更新表示为：

$$
h_j^{(l+1)} = \sum_{m=1}^{M} \sum_{(i \rightarrow j) \in \mathcal{E}_c^{(t)}} \alpha_{i \rightarrow j,m}^{(l)} \cdot W_{m,\tau} h_i^{(l)} \tag{31}
$$

**英文原文（第 7 段）**

> When a node simultaneously receives information from intra-sentence and inter-sentence causal edges, we further introduce type-level fusion:

**中文译文（第 7 段）**

当节点同时接收来自句内与跨句因果边的信息时，我们进一步引入类型级融合：

$$
\mathbf{h}_j^{(l+1)} = \eta \cdot \mathbf{h}_{j,\text{intra}}^{(l+1)} + (1 - \eta)\cdot \mathbf{h}_{j,\text{inter}}^{(l+1)}, \quad \eta \in (0,1) \tag{32}
$$

**英文原文（第 8 段）**

> where $\eta$ is a learnable parameter used to highlight the relative stability of intra-sentence causal relations in document understanding, while preserving the capability for cross-sentence causal reasoning.

**中文译文（第 8 段）**

其中 $\eta$ 是可学习参数，用于在文档理解中突出句内因果关系的相对稳定性，同时保留跨句因果推理的能力。

**英文原文（第 9 段）**

> To prevent graph structural features from overwhelming the node's own semantics and to enhance robustness in weak-structure scenarios, we adopt gated residual fusion:

**中文译文（第 9 段）**

为防止图结构特征淹没节点自身的语义，并增强模型在弱结构场景下的鲁棒性，我们采用门控残差融合：

$$
g_j = \sigma\left(\mathbf{W}_g\left[\mathbf{h}_{j,\mathrm{self}} \,\|\, \mathbf{h}_{j,\mathrm{graph}}\right]\right) \tag{33}
$$

$$
h_j^{(l+1)} = g_j \odot h_{j,\text{graph}} + (1 - g_j) \odot h_{j,\text{self}} \tag{34}
$$

**英文原文（第 10 段）**

> The gating vector $g_j$ is learned from the node itself, with LayerNorm subsequently applied to enhance training stability.

**中文译文（第 10 段）**

门控向量 $g_j$ 由节点自身学习得到，随后施加 LayerNorm 以增强训练稳定性。

## 附录 F 双层结构感知对比学习机制

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

> To strengthen the consistency of causal structures and boundary discrimination, we propose a dual-level iteration-aware contrastive learning strategy. In each iteration, positive and negative samples of events and event pairs are dynamically sampled based on the current causal graph to avoid mismatch between structure and representation caused by static sampling. Unlike existing methods (Gao et al., 2023), we introduce structural contrastive learning for the first time, which does not rely on semantics to construct positive and negative examples, making it suitable for scenarios where positive examples are scarce.

**中文译文（第 1 段）**

为增强因果结构的一致性与边界判别能力，我们提出一种双层、迭代感知的对比学习策略。在每一轮迭代中，基于当前因果图动态采样事件与事件对的正负样本，以避免静态采样造成的结构与表示失配。与现有方法（Gao et al., 2023）不同，我们首次引入结构对比学习，其正负样本的构建不依赖语义，因而适用于正样本稀缺的场景。

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> **Event-Level Structural Contrastive Learning:** Event-level contrastive learning is used to regularize the structural role consistency of individual events within the causal system. Given an event $e_i$, its structural representation $\mathbf{s}_i$ is composed of its 1-hop causal subgraph:

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

**事件级结构对比学习：** 事件级对比学习用于正则化因果系统中单个事件的结构角色一致性。给定事件 $e_i$，其结构表示 $\mathbf{s}_i$ 由其 1 跳因果子图构成：

$$
\mathbf{s}_i = \left[\mathbf{h}_i \,\|\, \frac{1}{|N(i)|}\sum_{j \in N(i)}\mathbf{h}_j \,\|\, \frac{1}{|N(i)|}\sum_{(i,j)} w_{ij}\right] \tag{35}
$$

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> where $\mathbf{h}_i$ is the representation of the event itself, $N(i)$ is the set of neighboring events, and $w_{ij}$ is the edge confidence in the current iteration. If $N(i) = \emptyset$, the neighbor and edge weight parts are set to zero. The contrastive vector is obtained after a learnable mapping and normalization:

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

其中 $\mathbf{h}_i$ 是事件自身的表示，$N(i)$ 为邻居事件集合，$w_{ij}$ 是当前迭代中的边置信度。若 $N(i) = \emptyset$，则邻居部分与边权重部分均置为零。对比向量经可学习映射与归一化后得到：

$$
\mathbf{z}_i = \text{Normalize}(\text{Proj}(\mathbf{s}_i)) \tag{36}
$$

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> The causal roles of events are implicitly induced by the document-level causal graph (e.g., cause-like / effect-like). For each event acting as an anchor, positive samples are selected from events with consistent roles, integrating semantic and structural similarity:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

事件的因果角色由文档级因果图隐式诱导（例如类因 / 类果）。对于每个作为锚点的事件，从角色一致的事件中选取正样本，并综合语义相似度与结构相似度：

$$
\operatorname{sim}_{\text{pos}}(i, j) = \cos(\mathbf{h}_i, \mathbf{h}_j) \cdot \frac{1}{1 + |\deg(i) - \deg(j)|} \tag{37}
$$

**英文原文（第 5 段）**

> Negative samples include: (1) Hard negative samples, which are the most structurally similar events selected from those with opposite roles; (2) Randomly sampled easy negative samples. The event-level contrastive loss employs a weighted InfoNCE:

**中文译文（第 5 段）**

负样本包括：(1) 困难负样本，即从角色相反的事件中选取的结构最相似的事件；(2) 随机采样的简单负样本。事件级对比损失采用加权 InfoNCE：

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{event}} = -\log \frac{\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_i, \mathbf{z}_i^+)/\tau)}{\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_i, \mathbf{z}_i^+)/\tau) + 2\exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_i, \mathbf{z}_i^{\text{hard-}})/\tau) + \exp(\cos(\mathbf{z}_i, \mathbf{z}_i^{\text{rand-}})/\tau)} \tag{38}
$$

**英文原文（第 6 段）**

> This objective imposes structural role consistency regularization on event representations without directly predicting causal relations.

**中文译文（第 6 段）**

该目标对事件表示施加结构角色一致性正则，而不直接预测因果关系。

**英文原文（第 7 段）**

> **Event-Pair-Level Structural Contrastive Learning:** To explicitly align structural representations with causal discrimination boundaries, we introduce event-pair-level structural contrastive learning, modeling causal relationships as compact relational units in a joint embedding space. For an event pair $(e_i, e_j)$, its pair representation is defined as:

**中文译文（第 7 段）**

**事件对级结构对比学习：** 为使结构表示与因果判别边界显式对齐，我们引入事件对级结构对比学习，将因果关系建模为联合嵌入空间中的紧凑关系单元。对于事件对 $(e_i, e_j)$，其配对表示定义为：

$$
\mathbf{h}_{ij} = [\,\mathbf{z}_i \,\|\, \mathbf{z}_j \,\|\, |\mathbf{z}_i - \mathbf{z}_j| \,\|\, \mathbf{z}_i \odot \mathbf{z}_j\,] \tag{39}
$$

**英文原文（第 8 段）**

> where $\mathbf{z}_i, \mathbf{z}_j$ are structured event embeddings refined by the graph. Let $\mathcal{P}^{+}$ and $\mathcal{P}^{-}$ denote the sets of causal and non-causal event pairs, respectively. For any non-causal pair, its hardness is defined as:

**中文译文（第 8 段）**

其中 $\mathbf{z}_i, \mathbf{z}_j$ 是经图结构细化后的事件嵌入。令 $\mathcal{P}^{+}$ 与 $\mathcal{P}^{-}$ 分别表示因果事件对集合与非因果事件对集合。对于任意非因果事件对，其困难程度定义为：

$$
\operatorname{hardness}(i, j) = \max_{(k, l) \in \mathcal{P}^{+}} \cos(\mathbf{z}_{ij}, \mathbf{z}_{kl}) \cdot (1 + w_{ij}) \tag{40}
$$

**英文原文（第 9 段）**

> The top-K pairs are selected as hard negatives $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{hard}}^{-}$. The selection of positive and negative samples is as follows:

**中文译文（第 9 段）**

选取困难度最高的前 K 个事件对作为困难负样本 $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{hard}}^{-}$。正、负样本的选取如下：

$$
\mathbf{z}_{ij}^{+} = \arg \max_{(k, l) \in \mathcal{P}^{+} \setminus (i, j)} \cos(\mathbf{z}_{ij}, \mathbf{z}_{kl}) \tag{41}
$$

$$
\mathbf{z}_{ij}^{-} = \arg \max_{(k, l) \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{hard}}^{-}} \cos(\mathbf{z}_{ij}, \mathbf{z}_{kl}) \tag{42}
$$

**英文原文（第 10 段）**

> A margin-based ranking loss is adopted:

**中文译文（第 10 段）**

采用基于间隔的排序损失：

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}} = \sum_{(i, j) \in \mathcal{P}^{+}} \max\left(0,\ \gamma + \cos(\mathbf{z}_{ij}, \mathbf{z}_{ij}^{-}) - \cos(\mathbf{z}_{ij}, \mathbf{z}_{ij}^{+})\right) \tag{43}
$$

**英文原文（第 11 段）**

> This objective explicitly pushes away non-causal structures that are most similar to causal ones, thereby strengthening the discrimination boundary and suppressing representation collapse. The structural contrastive loss is enabled only after the causal graph has initially converged and is introduced gradually through weight scheduling to reduce the interference of early noisy structures on the main task.

**中文译文（第 11 段）**

该目标显式地将与因果结构最相似的非因果结构推开，从而强化判别边界并抑制表示坍缩。结构对比损失仅在因果图初步收敛后启用，并通过权重调度逐步引入，以减少早期噪声结构对主任务的干扰。

## 附录 H 超参数设置 Hyperparameter Settings

**英文原文（第 1 段）**

> The model utilizes BERT-base-uncased as the encoder with a hidden dimension of 768. Event representations are obtained by performing mean pooling on the vectors of all tokens within their mention spans. To ensure reproducibility, the random seed is fixed at 209.

**中文译文（第 1 段）**

模型采用 BERT-base-uncased 作为编码器，隐藏维度为 768。事件表示通过对事件提及跨度内所有 token 的向量进行平均池化得到。为保证可复现性，随机种子固定为 209。

**英文原文（第 2 段）**

> **Dynamic Causal Graph Encoder:** We construct a dynamic heterogeneous causal graph comprising both intra-sentence and inter-sentence edges. Based on Graph Attention Networks, we design a causal-aware heterogeneous graph attention encoder that executes relation-specific multi-head attention calculations through dynamic edge weight adjustment and gated residual fusion mechanisms, facilitating iterative optimization of the document-level causal structure. The fusion weights for intra-sentence and inter-sentence relations are set to 0.7 and 0.3, respectively. The output dimension of the graph encoder is 768, with 4 attention heads. The graph structure optimization is controlled via a self-iteration mechanism, with a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 9 iterations. The structural convergence threshold is set to 2.

**中文译文（第 2 段）**

**动态因果图编码器：** 我们构建一个包含句内边与跨句边的动态异质因果图。基于图注意力网络，我们设计了一种因果感知的异构图注意力编码器，通过动态边权重调整与门控残差融合机制执行关系特定的多头注意力计算，从而支持文档级因果结构的迭代优化。句内关系与跨句关系的融合权重分别设为 0.7 与 0.3。图编码器的输出维度为 768，注意力头数为 4。图结构优化由自迭代机制控制，最少迭代 2 次，最多迭代 9 次。结构收敛阈值设为 2。

**英文原文（第 3 段）**

> **Classification and Training Objectives:** We consider two tasks: causal existence identification (CAUSE/NONE) and causal role classification (CAUSE/EFFECT/NONE). In the role classification task, the weights for the CAUSE and EFFECT categories are set to $\alpha = 0.75$, while the weight for the NONE category is $1 - \alpha$.

**中文译文（第 3 段）**

**分类与训练目标：** 我们考虑两个任务：因果关系存在性识别（CAUSE/NONE）与因果角色分类（CAUSE/EFFECT/NONE）。在角色分类任务中，CAUSE 与 EFFECT 类别的权重设为 $\alpha = 0.75$，而 NONE 类别的权重为 $1 - \alpha$。

**英文原文（第 4 段）**

> The overall training objective is:

**中文译文（第 4 段）**

整体训练目标为：

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cls}} + \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\text{event}} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{pair}} \tag{44}
$$

**英文原文（第 5 段）**

> where $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cls}}$ is implemented using Focal Loss with a focusing factor $\gamma = 2.0$ to alleviate class imbalance. To reinforce structural consistency, we introduce a dual-level structure-aware contrastive learning mechanism. For event-level contrastive loss, the temperature parameter is $\tau = 0.07$, the weight is $\lambda_1 = 0.1$, and it is introduced via an epoch-level warmup of 3. The event-pair-level contrastive loss is activated starting from epoch 10, with its weight $\lambda_2$ linearly increasing from 0.05. Edge weights across iterations are updated via EMA with a smoothing coefficient of 0.6.
